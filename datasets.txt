DATASETS USED FOR THE PAPER
===========================

1. nuScenes mini
   Official download:
   https://www.nuscenes.org/nuscenes

   Expected extracted location:
   data/nuscenes/v1.0-mini/

   Used for official mAP and NDS evaluation.

2. PandaSet
   Official download:
   https://scale.com/open-datasets/pandaset

   Expected extracted location:
   data/pandaset/

   Used for the six-camera hold-out center-matching experiment
   and the range and occupancy analysis.

The included result JSON files and hold-out information pickle
allow the saved predictions to be evaluated without downloading
the raw datasets again.

Raw datasets and model checkpoints are not included in this repository.
The original model repositories and compatible configurations are
needed to repeat inference or training from scratch.

KITTI, WoodScape, and the custom vehicle recordings are not used
in the reported experiments in this repository.
